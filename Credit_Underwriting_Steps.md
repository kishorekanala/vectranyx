# Credit Underwriting Steps

## Input
- Business Plan

## Workflow

### 1. Pre-screening & KYC (Quick Kill)
Bank pulls founder's personal and business credit reports (like CIBIL) to check for past defaults. They also check if business falls onto the bank’s "negative list" (highly volatile industries they refuse to finance). If you fail here, the application is rejected within 48 hours.

### 1. Feasibility Study

#### Market Feasibility
**Definition:** People want what you are selling at the price you need to charge

- Identify Target Audience
- Competitor Analysis
- Market Saturation & Trends

#### Technical Feasibility
**Definition:** Assesses the capability to create the product or deliver the service

- Technology & Equipment
- Expertise
- Scalability

#### Operational Feasibility
**Definition:** Evaluates how the business will run

- Supply Chain Logistics
- Facilities
- Human Resources

#### Legal & Regulatory Feasibility
**Definition:** Critical factor to run the business

- Licenses & Property
- Intellectual Property
- Insurance & Liability

#### Schedule Feasibility
**Definition:** Assesses if the timeline to launch is realistic

- Go-to-Market Speed
- Seasonality

---

### 2. 5 Cs of Credit Framework

Convert the feasibility study into a quantitative risk score using the 5 Cs of Credit:

#### Capacity
**Focus:** Scrutinize microeconomic feasibility

- Break-even analysis
- Projected cash flow and profit margins
- Stress test: Example: If raw material costs jump 20%, can they still cover monthly loan payment?

#### Conditions
**Focus:** Evaluates macroeconomic factors and market feasibility

- Are interest rates rising?
- Is the industry shrinking?
- Are supply chains for your raw materials globally disrupted?
- *Note:* If macroeconomic conditions are poor, banks will still lend but at higher interest rates or require a shorter payback period.

#### Capital
**Focus:** Bank checks startup costs and your contribution

- Example: You are asking $500,000 to build a manufacturing facility but only putting $10,000 of your own money → Bank will reject it
- Banks want your financial ruin tied to the business's failure so you are motivated to success

#### Collateral
**Focus:** Evaluates technical and operational feasibility

- Are you buying specialized machinery or real estate?
- The bank will appraise these assets
- If your loan is mostly for intangible things (like marketing or software development) → Banks view it as highly risky and may require a personal guarantee

#### Character
**Focus:** Evaluate technical expertise, team's personal credit history, and legal/regulatory compliance

- History of bankruptcy → Score plummets
- Lack of experience in the industry → Score plummets
- Strong track record & compliance → Score improves

---

## Output

**Credit Risk Rating (CRR) Scorecard**

Each of the 5 Cs is given a weight and combined to arrive at a Credit Risk Rating (CRR) scorecard.

- **Score Below Threshold:** Loan is automatically denied
- **Score Passes Threshold:** The score dictates your interest rate and loan terms
