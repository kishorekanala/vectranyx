Input - 
Business plan

Activities - 

1. Feasibility Study -
	Market Feasibility - People want what you are selling at the price you need to charge
		- Identify Target Audience
		- Competitor Analysis
		- Market Saturation & Trends
	Technical Feasibility - Assesses the capability to create the product or deliver the service
		- Technology & Equipment
		- Expertise
		- Scalability
	Operational Feasibility - Evaluates how the business will run
		- Supply Chain Logistics
		- Facilities
		- Human Resources
	Legal & Regulatory Feasibility  - Critical factor to run the business
		- Licenses & Property
		- Intellectual Property 
		- Insurance & Liability
	Schedule Feasibility - Assesses if the timeline to launch is realistic
		- Go-to-Market-Speed
		- Seasonality

2. Use 5 Cs of Credit framework to use the feasibility study and convert it into quantitive risk score.
	Capacity - Scrutinize Microeconomic feasibility. Break-even analysis, projected cash flow, and profit margins. Perform stress test. Example: if raw material costs jump 20% can they still cover monthly loan payment.
	Conditions - Evaluates Macroeconomic factors and Market feasibility. Example: Are interest rates rising? Is the industry shrinking? Are supply chains for your raw materials globally disrupted? If macroeconomic conditions are poor, bank will still lend but at higher interest rate or require a shorter payback period.
	Capital - Bank checks startup costs and your contribution. Example: you are asking $500,000 to build a manufacturing facility but only putting $10,000 of your own money, the bank will reject it. They want your financial ruin tied to the business's failure so you are motivated to success.
	Collateral - Evaluates technical  and operational  feasibility. Are you buying specialised machinery or real estate? The bank will appraise these assets. If your loan is mostly for intangible things (like marketing or software development) they will view the loan a highly risky and may require a personal guarantee.
	Character - Evaluate your technical expertise, your team's personal credit history and your legal/regulatory compliance. If you have a history of bankruptcy or lack experience in the industry you are entering, your score will plummets.
\
	Each of the 5 C's is given a weight and arrived at CRR (Credit Risk Rating) scorecard. If score falls below their threshold , the loan is automatically denied. If it passes, the score dictates your interest rate and loan terms.
