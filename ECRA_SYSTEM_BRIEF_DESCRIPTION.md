# Enterprise Credit and Risk Assessment (ECRA) System
ECRA System is an automated, auditable decision intelligence platform for lending and investment decisions, with continuous post‑disbursal and post‑investment monitoring.

## Elevator Pitch
The Enterprise Credit and Risk Assessment (ECRA) System ingests a company’s business profile and plan, synthesizes macro/micro risk vectors, and delivers an auditable credit and investment decision package with a weighted risk scoreboard, stress‑test simulations, and actionable early‑warning signals.

## Brief Summary
The ECRA System converts business profiles and plans into instrument-grade evaluations by mapping sector/resource vectors, verifying key assumptions, and scoring geopolitical, supply‑chain, financial, pricing, and regulatory risks. Outputs include a weighted credit rating, supply‑chain resilience analysis, scenario stress tests (DSCR/ICR, liquidity runway), and a prioritized remediation plan to support fund/do‑not‑fund decisions for both lenders and investors, plus ongoing portfolio monitoring.


## Detailed Description
The Enterprise Credit and Risk Assessment System is a modular underwriting and monitoring platform that programmatically ingests a Business Profile and Business Plan, decomposes required resources (hardware, software, human capital), and synthesizes a factor vector across systemic macroeconomic and entity‑specific microeconomic domains. Specialized ADK agents perform assumption audits, market/ESG/regulatory checks, financial-model validation, and scenario-based stress‑testing to produce an auditable Executive Credit & Risk Scoreboard (geopolitical, supply‑chain, capital structure, margin integrity, regulatory). The system generates lender- and investor-ready deliverables—scorecards, narrative findings, covenant breach probabilities, capital buffer recommendations, and early warning triggers—enabling disciplined pre‑disbursal and pre‑investment decisions as well as continuous portfolio surveillance.
