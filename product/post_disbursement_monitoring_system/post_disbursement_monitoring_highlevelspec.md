The Summary of the Bank's View
To a lender, Microeconomic factors (your cash flow, your break-even, your costs) determine IF they will lend to you.

Macroeconomic factors (inflation, interest rates, GDP trends) determine HOW MUCH it will cost you and WHAT TERMS they will force you to accept.


*The Problem:* Underwriters currently stress-test in static Excel files. They guess at inflation impacts.
*Your Value Add:* Build an engine that ingests real-time macroeconomic APIs (live commodity prices, inflation indices, central bank interest rates) and automatically lays them over the borrower's projected cash flow.

•⁠  ⁠The Feature: A dashboard for the Credit Committee where they can drag a slider labeled "Steel Prices +20%" and instantly see how it changes the specific borrower's Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR). You turn static math into a dynamic, predictive engine.

## 2. API-Driven Post-Disbursement Monitoring (The Early Warning System)

*The Problem:* Step 7 (Monitoring) is broken. Banks wait 90 days for a borrower to submit a quarterly PDF report. By the time the bank realizes the borrower is failing, the money is already gone.
*Your Value Add:* "Continuous Underwriting." Your software integrates directly via API into the borrower’s daily operational software (e.g., QuickBooks, Tally, Xero, or their bank feeds) once the loan is approved.

•⁠  ⁠The Feature: If a borrower's cash buffer drops below the required bank covenant on a random Tuesday, or if they lose their biggest client, your system flags the bank immediately—long before the quarterly report is due. This drastically reduces the bank's Non-Performing Assets (NPAs).

## 3. Alternative Data for "Thin-File" Businesses

*The Problem:* In Step 1 (Pre-Screening), banks reject thousands of profitable small businesses simply because they don't have a 5-year traditional credit history (a "thin file").
*Your Value Add:* Incorporate alternative data points into the pre-screening algorithm to prove operational capacity.

•⁠  ⁠The Feature: Your solution ingests digital exhaust: GST/tax return consistency, digital payments volume (Stripe/Razorpay data), supply chain ERP data, and even customer review sentiment. You help the bank safely say "yes" to a lucrative market segment their legacy systems automatically reject.
