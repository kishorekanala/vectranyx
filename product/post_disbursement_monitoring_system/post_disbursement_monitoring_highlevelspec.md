How it hits: The bank will monitor how your microeconomics hold up against macro realities over time. If a tight labor market forces you to raise wages to keep employees, but your revenue stays flat, the bank will see your margins shrinking on your quarterly statements and may intervene.

The Summary of the Bank's View
To a lender, Microeconomic factors (your cash flow, your break-even, your costs) determine IF they will lend to you.

Macroeconomic factors (inflation, interest rates, GDP trends) determine HOW MUCH it will cost you and WHAT TERMS they will force you to accept.
