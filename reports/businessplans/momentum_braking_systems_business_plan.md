
# Momentum Braking Systems: Business Plan

--- 

## 0. Business Name

**Momentum Braking Systems**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Momentum Braking Systems is a proposed new venture to manufacture high-quality, reliable, and cost-effective braking systems for the Indian passenger vehicle market. The business will cater to both Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and the aftermarket. The core business opportunity lies in the rapidly growing Indian automotive market, coupled with increasing consumer and regulatory focus on vehicle safety. Our value proposition is to provide world-class safety and performance at a competitive price point, leveraging a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in India. We project a 5-year goal to capture a significant share of the aftermarket and secure at least two major OEM contracts.

---

## 2. Company Profile

*   **Mission:** To be the premier Indian manufacturer of automotive braking systems, enhancing road safety by delivering products of superior quality, reliability, and performance.
*   **Vision:** To see a future where every vehicle on Indian roads is equipped with safe, dependable braking technology.
*   **Core Values:**
    *   **Safety First:** Our uncompromising commitment to the safety of our end-users.
    *   **Quality Excellence:** Adherence to the highest standards of manufacturing and quality control.
    *   **Innovation:** Continuous improvement and development of new braking technologies.
    *   **Customer Partnership:** Building long-term, collaborative relationships with our clients.

---

## 3. Products & Services Description

We will manufacture a comprehensive range of braking components and systems.

*   **Product Portfolio:**
    *   **Brake Assemblies:** Disc brake calipers, pads, and rotors.
    *   **Brake Hydraulics:** Master cylinders, wheel cylinders, and brake boosters.
    *   **Safety Systems:** Anti-lock Braking System (ABS) and Electronic Stability Control (ESC) modules.

*   **Business Model:**
    *   **Phase 1:** Focus on the aftermarket by supplying high-demand replacement parts like brake pads and rotors.
    *   **Phase 2:** Expand into OEM supply, providing complete braking systems to passenger vehicle manufacturers.

*   **Monetization:** Revenue will be generated through B2B sales to automotive OEMs and a tiered distribution network for the aftermarket.

---

## 4. Marketing & Sales Strategy

*   **Go-to-Market Plan:**
    *   **OEMs:** A dedicated corporate sales team will build relationships with the procurement and R&D departments of major auto manufacturers in India (e.g., Tata Motors, Mahindra, Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai).
    *   **Aftermarket:** Establish a multi-level distribution network targeting wholesalers, retailers, and large service garages across India.
*   **Distribution Channels:** A central warehousing facility will feed regional distribution hubs in major metro areas to ensure product availability and reduce lead times.
*   **Pricing Strategy:** Competitive pricing positioned between premium international brands and lower-quality unorganized players. We will compete on a platform of superior quality and value.

---

## 5. Operations Plan

*   **Facility:** A modern manufacturing plant will be established in a major Indian automotive hub like Pune or Chennai. The facility will house casting, machining, assembly, and rigorous testing lines.
*   **Logistics & Supply Chain:** We will implement a Just-In-Time (JIT) supply chain for our OEM clients. Raw materials such as pig iron, steel, and friction compounds will be sourced from certified local and global suppliers.
*   **Utilities:** The plant will require a consistent supply of high-load electricity, water for cooling processes, and other standard industrial utilities.

---

## 6. Management Team

*   **CEO:** An experienced leader with over 20 years in the Indian automotive components industry.
*   **COO:** A manufacturing expert with a track record in setting up and running lean automotive production facilities.
*   **Head of Sales:** A well-networked professional with established relationships within automotive OEMs and the aftermarket distribution chain.
*   **CFO:** A finance professional with experience in project finance, and managing financial operations in a manufacturing environment.

## 8. Funding Request

*   **Requested Facility:** A seed funding round of **INR 150 Crore**.
*   **Deployment of Funds:**
    *   **Capital Expenditure (70%):** Land acquisition, plant construction, and machinery purchase.
    *   **Go-to-Market (15%):** Marketing, sales team hiring, and brand building.
    *   **Working Capital (15%):** Inventory, and operational expenses for the first year.

---

## 9. Risk Management Plan

*   **Market Risk:** Intense competition from established international and local players.
    *   **Mitigation:** Focus on superior quality, competitive pricing, and strong customer relationships.
*   **Execution Risk:** Potential delays in plant commissioning or quality control issues.
    *   **Mitigation:** Hiring an experienced project management team and implementing a robust Quality Management System (QMS) from day one.
*   **Financial Risk:** Volatility in raw material prices.
    *   **Mitigation:** Long-term supplier contracts and strategic hedging.

---

## 10. Dependencies

*   **Hardware:** Sourcing of high-precision CNC machines, casting furnaces, and advanced testing equipment.
*   **Software:** Implementation of an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system (e.g., SAP) and CAD/CAM software for design and manufacturing.
*   **Suppliers:** Establishing reliable partnerships with suppliers of high-grade raw materials.

---

## 11. Team Structure & Expertise

*   **Structure:** A functional organizational structure with clear divisions for Production, Quality Assurance, R&D, Sales & Marketing, and Finance.
*   **Hiring Priorities:** Immediate hiring priorities include a Plant Head, Quality Control engineers, and a senior OEM Sales Manager.
*   **Talent Development:** A continuous training program will be implemented to keep the workforce updated on the latest manufacturing techniques and quality standards.
