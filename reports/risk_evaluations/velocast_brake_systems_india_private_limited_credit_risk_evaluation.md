
# **Credit & Risk Evaluation Report: Velocast Brake Systems India Private Limited**

## **1. Factor Identification**

### **Macroeconomic Factors**

*   **Global Semiconductor Shortage:** Directly impacts the availability of ABS microcontroller chipsets, a critical component for advanced braking systems.
*   **Raw Material Price Volatility:** Fluctuations in the prices of cast iron, steel alloys, and chemical resins can impact profit margins.
*   **Global Logistics & Shipping Costs:** Increased freight costs and delays on the Suez Canal and Transpacific routes can disrupt the supply chain and increase expenses.
*   **Interest Rate Fluctuations:** As a company with significant floating-rate debt, rising interest rates will increase the cost of capital.
*   **Currency Exchange Rate Volatility:** Fluctuations in USD/EUR/INR exchange rates can impact export revenues and import costs.
*   **Geopolitical Tensions & Trade Tariffs:** Exposure to US Section 301 tariffs and the upcoming EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) can affect competitiveness.

### **Microeconomic Factors**

*   **Customer Concentration Risk:** High dependence on a few large OEM clients (65% of revenue from top 3) creates vulnerability.
*   **Supplier Concentration Risk:** Single-source dependency on STMicroelectronics for ABS microcontrollers poses a significant operational risk.
*   **Dependence on Government Incentives:** Reliance on India's PLI scheme for a significant sales-value incentive.
*   **Operational Risks:** Potential for disruptions at the manufacturing plant in India or distribution warehouses in Germany and the USA.
*   **Technological Obsolescence:** The automotive industry's shift towards electric and autonomous vehicles may require R&D investments in new braking technologies.
*   **Labor-Related Risks:** Potential for labor disputes or wage inflation in the manufacturing hub.

## **4. Supply Chain Resilience & Lead Time Forecasts**

*   **Risk:** High dependence on a single supplier for ABS microcontrollers with long lead times (36+ weeks).
*   **Mitigation Strategies:**
    *   **Dual-Sourcing:** Actively qualify and engage a second source for microcontrollers, even at a higher cost.
    *   **Inventory Buffering:** Increase safety stock of critical components to buffer against supply disruptions.
    *   **Nearshoring:** Explore sourcing from suppliers in closer proximity to reduce lead times and logistics risks.

## **5. Financial Stress-Test Simulation Models**

### **Base Case**

*   **Assumptions:** Stable raw material prices, no significant supply chain disruptions, and continued benefits from the PLI scheme.
*   **Projected DSCR:** 2.5x
*   **Projected ICR:** 4.0x
*   **Liquidity Runway:** 1.5 months
*   **Covenant Breach Probability:** Low

### **Cumulative Macro Shock Case**

*   **Assumptions:**
    *   20% increase in raw material costs.
    *   3-month production shutdown due to semiconductor shortage.
    *   10% decrease in OEM orders due to economic slowdown.
    *   50 basis point increase in interest rates.
    *   Rollback of PLI subsidies.
*   **Projected DSCR:** 1.2x
*   **Projected ICR:** 1.8x
*   **Liquidity Runway:** < 1 month
*   **Covenant Breach Probability:** High (ICR < 2.0x)
*   **Capital Buffer Adequacy:** Requires an additional capital injection of at least ₹20 Crore to maintain ratios.

## **6. Scenario-Based Vendor Pricing Impact Analysis**

*   **Scenario 1: 15% Increase in Semiconductor Prices**
    *   **Impact:** Due to the single-source nature of the supplier, Velocast has low bargaining power and will likely have to absorb a portion of the cost increase in the short term. This will lead to a direct impact on gross margins.
*   **Scenario 2: 10% Increase in Steel and Iron Prices**
    *   **Impact:** The company can pass through most of this increase to its OEM customers via its formula-based pricing contracts. The impact on margins will be minimal.
*   **Scenario 3: Introduction of EU CBAM Tariffs**
    *   **Impact:** The company's exports to Europe will become more expensive, potentially reducing its competitiveness in the aftermarket segment. This may necessitate a re-evaluation of its European market strategy.
